RSAF heat-strain shirt, size M. The shirt is a cloth simulation on a moving mannequin: pick a fit and a motion, and each sensor's live skin gap is reported. Drag any sensor on the 3D shirt. The shirt colours show how strongly the consolidated literature review supports each spot for the selected sensor, and the flats below redraw with dimensions from the back neck point (BNP), centre back (CB) and centre front (CF).

Drag a sensor to move it; drag empty space to rotate.
